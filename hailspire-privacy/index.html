Privacy Policy for Hailspire

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Hailspire is made by Striking Games. This policy explains what the game keeps on your phone and what the Google services inside it collect.

Striking Games runs no server and receives nothing that identifies you. Google gives us only totals (installs, ad earnings) and crash reports with the phone model and Android version, never your name or account. Hailspire has no accounts of its own, no in-app purchases and no analytics, and you can play without signing in to anything. If you email us, we use your address only to reply.

1. What the Game Keeps on Your Phone

Your progress, coins and settings are saved in the game's own storage on your phone, with a few small things the game uses itself: your phone's model name, graphics chip and screen resolution (so that a save moved to a new phone starts with fresh settings, and the game remembers which graphics settings run smoothly on this phone), how many ads you watched today, whether you turned reminders and Play Games on, and, only while reminders are on, the times of day your last 14 sessions started (to pick a good time for a reminder). Uninstalling the game deletes all of it from the phone.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows a rewarded ad only when you choose to watch one (DOUBLE IT, ONE MORE CHANCE), and small banners at the top of some results and game-over screens. To have an ad ready, the game asks Google for one in the background on results screens and the menu, even if you never watch it.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. Google, and the ad companies Google works with, collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID and app set ID, your IP address (and from it an approximate location), how you interact with ads, and crash and performance information from the ads library, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. They may use it to show ads based on your interests, including what you do in other apps and websites.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices first, and you can change them at any time with PRIVACY CHOICES in the game's Settings. Anywhere, you can see and turn off ad personalisation at myadcenter.google.com, and reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings. The ads are limited to content rated for teens.

3. Google Play Games

If you tap the trophy and sign in to Google Play Games, the game sends your scores to its leaderboards, unlocks its achievements, and keeps a copy of your progress and coins in Play Games so that another phone can continue it (your settings, phone model and session times stay on the phone). It reconnects each time the game starts; you can disconnect it in your Play Games settings. Depending on your Play Games profile settings, Google shows your Play Games name, picture and scores to other players on the leaderboards, and the game shows your friends' scores only if you choose to share your friends list with it. This is handled by Google under your Google account.

4. Reminders

If you turn reminders on, the game schedules at most one quiet notification a day on your phone. The game asks you first, then Android asks for permission (Android 13 and later). The reminders themselves are made on the phone; nothing about them is sent to us or to any server. You can turn them off with REMINDERS in the game's Settings, or in Android's settings.

5. Backup

If Android's backup to your Google account is on, Android may back up the game's save (everything in section 1), and nothing else of the game, so that a new phone can restore your progress. That backup is handled by Google under your account and never reaches us.

6. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: ads and Google Play Games
  • Advertising ID: ads
  • Notifications and starting after a restart: reminders
  • Keeping the phone awake briefly and background work (wake lock, foreground service): used by Google's ads library and the reminder scheduler
  • Vibration: short ticks when you fire and press buttons (VIBRATION in Settings turns it off)

7. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob, for ads
  • Google User Messaging Platform, for ad consent and privacy choices
  • Google Play Games Services, for leaderboards, achievements and saving your game to Play Games

Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

8. Data Sharing and Sale

Striking Games never receives your personal information and does not sell it. The game's ads work by letting Google, and the ad companies Google works with, collect the information in section 2 and use it under Google's policies. Some US state laws count using it for personalised ads as "sharing". To stop it, delete your advertising ID in Android's settings, or use PRIVACY CHOICES in the EU, the UK and Switzerland.

9. How Long It Is Kept, and Deleting It

The save stays on your phone until you uninstall the game. The Play Games copy and the Android backup stay in your Google account until you delete them: Play Games scores, achievements and the saved game in the Google Play Games settings, the backup in your Google account's backup settings (Google also removes a phone's backup after a period without use). Google keeps ad information as its privacy policy says. Emails to us are kept only as long as needed to answer them. Google's services send everything over encrypted connections. Striking Games holds no copy of any of it.

10. Your Rights (EU, UK, Switzerland)

Striking Games, the developer of Hailspire, is responsible for this game; contact strikinggamesunity@gmail.com. In these regions the ads use your consent, given in Google's form, which you can change at any time with PRIVACY CHOICES. Play Games and backup run because you turn them on, and the save on your phone is needed to play. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to its use, and complain to your data protection authority. We hold no data about you, so for ads and Play Games the quickest route is Google's own tools (myaccount.google.com, myadcenter.google.com); write to us and we'll help. Google may process this information in the United States and other countries, under the safeguards in its privacy policy.

11. US State Privacy Laws

The information in section 2 (advertising ID, ad interactions, approximate location) is collected by Google for ads. You can ask us what we hold (nothing) or to delete it, and opt out of its use for personalised ads by deleting your advertising ID. We will never treat you differently for using these rights.

12. Children

Hailspire is made for players 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. The game itself collects nothing, and Striking Games does not knowingly receive information from children. If you think a child under 13 has played with Play Games or ads on, you can delete their Play Games data and advertising ID as described above, or contact us.
